# Innewgen Brand Experience

Analyze and deeply audit my existing website: innewgen.com. Research modern, smaller creative agencies, startups, and digital businesses, especially socia.ph  and bryllim.com . Study their website structures, visual identities, animations, interactions, page layouts, and what makes their brands stand out. Identify what works, what doesn't, and what I can learn from them.
Based on your findings, redesign my website to feel distinctive, premium, modern, interactive, and intentionally designed. Avoid generic AI-generated layouts, repetitive card grids, predictable hero sections, and overused website templates. I want a strong visual identity, creative transitions, smooth animations, engaging scroll effects, and thoughtful micro-interactions inspired by the quality and energy of Socia, without copying their design.
Design requirements:
Preserve and build upon my existing logo, brand identity, and color palette.
Use light mode as the default, with a working light/dark mode toggle.
Create a distinctive visual direction with strong typography, creative compositions, layered elements, and purposeful motion.
Build a complete multi-page website with a clear structure, dedicated subpages, and cohesive navigation, inspired by the depth of Socia's website.
Improve the user journey, messaging, conversion flow, responsiveness, accessibility, and overall user experience.
Make every animation and interaction feel intentional, polished, and smooth—not distracting or purely decorative.
Keep and improve my existing project inquiry form, including its six-step process: About You, Business, Challenges, Goals, Details, and Schedule. Preserve its key information, improve the visual experience, and ensure the form is easy to complete on desktop and mobile.
Workflow: First, audit my current website and research the reference sites. Identify the main weaknesses and propose a clear creative direction. Then implement the redesign directly in my existing project, preserving working functionality and improving the site beyond a simple visual refresh.
The final result should feel like a real, professionally art-directed brand—not another template. Be bold, distinctive, and intentional in every design decision.
